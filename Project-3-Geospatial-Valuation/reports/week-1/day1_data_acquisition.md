## Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** King County House Sales Dataset
- **Records:** 21,613
- **Features:** 21
- **Target Variable:** Price

The dataset contains property characteristics such as bedrooms, bathrooms, living area, lot size, construction year, renovation year, zipcode, latitude, and longitude, making it suitable for geospatial valuation modeling.

---

## Key Observations

- The dataset contains geographical coordinates for every property.
- House prices show a right-skewed distribution with several high-value outliers.
- Property locations are concentrated within King County, making them suitable for neighborhood-based spatial analysis.
- The available latitude and longitude information will enable graph construction and spatial feature engineering in later stages.
